# AfterMath — Demo Script

## 60-Second Demo Flow

### 0:00–0:10 — Decision Setup

1. Open http://localhost:3000
2. Show the headline: "This home looks affordable today. What happens after
   you sign?"
3. Point to the risky preset (amber, selected by default)
4. Mention: Property 3.2B VND, teaser rate 6.8% → 11.5% after 12 months
5. Click "Reveal the aftermath"

### 0:10–0:30 — Live Pre-Mortem

6. Five agents appear sequentially with blue spinner → green/red status:
   - **Terms Agent:** "Rate resets from 6.8% to 11.5% after 12 months.
     Payment increases by 38%."
   - **Cashflow Agent:** "Monthly deficit of ₫6.3M after the rate reset."
   - **Hidden Cost Agent:** "Hidden costs add ₫8M/month on top of the
     mortgage — 17% of income."
   - **Shock Agent:** "3-month income loss would leave only 2 months of
     runway."
   - **Decision Agent:** "HIGH REGRET RISK — 81/100"
7. Progress bar fills left-to-right as agents complete

### 0:30–0:45 — Aftermath Result

8. Score counts up to 81/100 (red, prominent)
9. Consequence timeline shows:
   - Month 1: Affordable intro period (green)
   - Month 13: Interest-rate reset (amber)
   - Month 19: Critical cash-flow turning point (red, pulsing)
10. Score breakdown shows all 7 risk factors with visible contributions

### 0:45–0:55 — Safer Alternative

11. Click "Find a safer version"
12. Score animates from 81 → 34
13. Before/after comparison shows reduced payment, longer fixed period,
    larger emergency fund
14. Updated timeline shows the safer scenario is manageable

### 0:55–1:00 — Share & Wrap

15. Click "Share" → share card modal appears
16. Mention: downloadable as SVG, copyable as text
17. All data is synthetic — not financial advice

## Tips

- Use browser zoom 100% on a 1440×900 display
- Presentation mode hides the top bar and disclaimer
- Replay button restarts the agent animation
- Restart button returns to the setup screen
