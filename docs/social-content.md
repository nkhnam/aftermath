## Medium Pitch (LinkedIn)

What if you could see the financial aftermath of a loan decision before
signing?

That's what AfterMath does. It's an AI Financial Pre-Mortem tool that
simulates the consequences most people don't consider:

→ Interest-rate resets that double your payment
→ Hidden housing costs excluded from the mortgage quote
→ Income disruption draining your emergency fund
→ The exact month where the decision becomes dangerous

Five agents analyze the scenario sequentially — Terms, Cashflow, Hidden
Costs, Shock, and Decision — each with visible findings, evidence, and
confidence scores.

The risky scenario scores 81/100 regret risk. The safer version? 34/100.

Every score contribution is transparent. No black box. No ML prediction.
Just deterministic, visible, synthetic scenario scoring.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Framer Motion.

## Key Talking Points

1. **Pre-mortem, not post-mortem** — catch the problem before you sign
2. **Transparent scoring** — every point is visible, no black box
3. **Five specialized agents** — each checks a different risk dimension
4. **Synthetic data** — clearly labeled, not real financial advice
5. **Critical turning point** — the month where it all goes wrong
6. **Safer alternative** — actionable changes that reduce risk by 47 points
